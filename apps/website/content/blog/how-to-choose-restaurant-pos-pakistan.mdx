# How to Choose a POS System for Your Pakistani Restaurant

Choosing the right Point of Sale (POS) software is one of the most critical decisions a restaurant owner will make. In Pakistan, generic international POS software often fails because it lacks support for local payment gateways like JazzCash, fails to handle internet outages, and cannot generate FBR tax invoices.

Here are the top 5 factors to consider when selecting a POS system for your Pakistani restaurant:

---

## 1. 100% Offline Capability

Internet drops in Pakistan are frequent. If your POS software relies entirely on a constant cloud connection, your counter will grind to a halt during rush hours. Ensure your POS has a robust local database that allows continuous billing offline and auto-syncs when connectivity returns.

---

## 2. JazzCash & EasyPaisa Payment Integration

Digital wallet usage in Pakistan has surged. Customers expect to pay via JazzCash or EasyPaisa QR codes without waiting for cashiers to manually double-check phone numbers.

---

## 3. FBR & Provincial Tax Compliance

Make sure the POS can calculate GST for dine-in vs takeaway and print certified FBR QR codes directly onto receipts.

---

## 4. Kitchen Display System (KDS) & KOT Routing

Paper kitchen order tickets (KOT) get lost or stained easily in fast-paced kitchens. Digital KDS screens route items directly to the grill, biryani, or beverage station with color-coded timers.

---

## 5. Local Urdu Language Support

Your floor staff and cashiers should be able to navigate the app in Urdu or Roman Urdu effortlessly, reducing training time to under 10 minutes.
