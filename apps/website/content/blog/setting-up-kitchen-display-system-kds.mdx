# Setting Up Kitchen Display System (KDS) for Your Restaurant

In high-volume kitchens, paper tickets get wet, lost, or misread. A **Kitchen Display System (KDS)** replaces paper tickets with digital screens mounted directly at preparation stations.

## Benefits of KDS in Pakistani Kitchens
* **Color-Coded Timers:** Orders turn yellow at 5 minutes and red at 10 minutes to signal kitchen delay.
* **Station Separation:** Tandoor orders go to the naan screen; karahi orders go to the chef screen.
* **One-Tap Bump:** Kitchen staff tap finished items to dispatch waiters immediately.
