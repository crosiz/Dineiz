# WhatsApp Ordering for Restaurants — How It Works and Why You Need It

In Pakistan, WhatsApp is the default messaging app used by millions daily. Customers prefer sending a quick WhatsApp message to order biryani or pizza over downloading a heavy third-party delivery app.

However, answering WhatsApp orders manually during peak rush hours often leads to missed messages, incorrect orders, and delayed delivery.

---

## The Solution: AI-Powered WhatsApp Ordering

Dineiz WhatsApp AI Bot turns your WhatsApp Business number into an automated 24/7 ordering kiosk:

1. **Natural Conversational AI:** Understands Roman Urdu and English (e.g. *"2 chicken biryani single aur 1 pepsi"*).
2. **Automated Menu Display:** Shows dish categories and price additions automatically.
3. **Instant JazzCash/EasyPaisa QR:** Sends payment links inside the chat and verifies payment receipts.
4. **Kitchen Display Sync:** Confirmed orders go straight to your POS and kitchen display screens without manual entry.
