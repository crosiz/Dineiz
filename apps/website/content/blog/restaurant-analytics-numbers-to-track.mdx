# Restaurant Analytics — 7 Numbers Every Owner Must Track

Running a successful restaurant requires keeping a close eye on key financial and operational metrics. Here are the 7 numbers you should track daily in your Dineiz Console:

1. **Daily Net Sales vs Gross Revenue**
2. **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) %**
3. **Average Ticket Size per Guest**
4. **Top 5 Profit Margin Dishes**
5. **Peak Hourly Sales Volume**
6. **Inventory Waste & Variance Rate**
7. **Customer Repeat Order Percentage**

Tracking these metrics in real-time allows you to adjust menu pricing, reduce food wastage, and optimize staff schedules. Explore [Dineiz Analytics Dashboard](https://dineiz.com/product/console).
