## 2. Candela RMS — Best for Retail Apparel & Footwear Chains

**Best For:** Clothing boutiques, shoe store chains, cosmetics, and multi-store retail.

Candela by Anisuma is one of the most established retail management systems in Pakistan. If you run a clothing brand or a multi-location shoe franchise, Candela offers mature supply chain management, matrix inventory (size, color, style), and warehouse distribution features.

### Pros:
* Mature matrix inventory management for apparel.
* Centralized warehouse replenishment and store transfers.
* Established presence with corporate training in Pakistan.

### Cons:
* Not designed for restaurants: No KOT printing, table management, or recipe ingredient depletion.
* Traditional Windows desktop interface that feels dated compared to modern web applications.
* Higher upfront licensing and annual maintenance costs.

**Best For:** Supermarkets, convenience stores, department stores, and pharmacies.

## 4. Mint POS — Traditional Desktop Restaurant System

**Best For:** Traditional standalone restaurants seeking desktop software.

Mint POS is a local restaurant management tool offering basic KOT routing, cashier drawers, and table numbers.

### Pros:

### Cons:

## Key Questions to Ask Before Buying Any POS in Pakistan

* **What happens when the internet drops?** If the software stops billing, walk away. Every restaurant in Pakistan requires 100% offline resilience.
* **Are there hidden fees?** Watch out for mandatory setup charges, hardware lock-in, and per-call support fees.
* **Can I monitor sales on my phone?** You shouldn't have to be physically present at the cash register to see today's revenue.
* **Does it integrate with local payment gateways and FBR?** Ensure the POS natively supports JazzCash, EasyPaisa, card split GST rates, and verified FBR invoice numbers.
