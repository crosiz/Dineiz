# Dhaba mein Billing Software Kaise Use Karein — Step by Step Guide

Agar aap Pakistan mein koi dhaba, chai hotel, ya chota khana counter chalatay hain, toh purana khata system ab khatam karein. Manual register par hisaab likhnay se paise ki chori aur ghalatiyan hoti hain.

**Dineiz Go** app se aap apnay mobile phone par 10 minute mein digital billing shuru kar saktay hain.

---

## Step 1: App Download Karein
Google Play Store se **Dineiz Go** free download karein aur apna phone number enter karkay OTP verify karein.

## Step 2: Apnay Khano Ke Naam Aur Qeemat Daalein
App mein *Menu Settings* kholain aur apnay khano ki qeemat daalein (maslan: Single Biryani - PKR 380, Chai - PKR 80).

## Step 3: Bluetooth Printer Connect Karein
Market se sasta Bluetooth thermal printer le kar 2 taps mein phone se connect karein.

## Step 4: Pehla Bill Banayein
Customer ke order par click karein aur *PRINT BILL* press karein. Receipt seconds mein print ho jaye gi!
