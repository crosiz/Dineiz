Staff theft is the silent killer of the restaurant industry. While owners often obsess over food costs and marketing budgets, a dishonest cashier or manager can bleed a profitable restaurant dry right under the owner's nose.

The problem usually isn't large-scale embezzlement; it is the "death by a thousand cuts." A voided receipt here, a discounted meal there.

Here are the most common ways staff steal from restaurants and how you can use a modern POS system like Dineiz to stop them immediately.

## 1. The "Voided Item" Scam

**How it works:**
A customer orders a pizza and pays in cash. The cashier hands them the food but never prints the final receipt. Once the customer leaves, the cashier goes into the POS, voids the pizza from the ticket, and pockets the cash. The system thinks the food was never sold.

**How Dineiz stops it:**
Dineiz includes strict **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**.
You can configure the system so that only a Manager (with a specific PIN code) can void an item *after* it has been sent to the kitchen. The cashier simply does not have the button available on their screen.

Furthermore, every single void is logged in an un-deletable "Void Report." If you see that Cashier Ali voided 15 items on a Tuesday night, you know exactly where to look.

## 2. The "Fake Discount" Trick

**How it works:**
A customer's bill is PKR 2,000. They pay PKR 2,000 in cash. The cashier then applies a "100% Friends & Family" discount or a "50% Employee Discount" to the ticket before closing it. The system records the sale as PKR 0 or PKR 1,000, and the cashier pockets the difference.

**How Dineiz stops it:**
Similar to voids, discounts can be locked behind a Manager PIN. But more importantly, Dineiz tracks discount usage by employee.

You can pull a weekly "Discount Report" on your owner's dashboard. If one specific cashier is applying 80% of all discounts in your restaurant, the data highlights the anomaly instantly.

## 3. The "No-Ring" Sale (Inventory Skimming)

**How it works:**
A customer buys a bottled water. The cashier takes the cash, gives the customer the water, and never punches the item into the POS at all.

**How Dineiz stops it:**
This is where **tight inventory integration** comes in.
Dineiz links your sales directly to your stock. If you started the day with 50 bottles of water, and the POS says you sold 10, you should have 40 left.

If you do a blind physical count at the end of the shift and find only 30 bottles left, you immediately know that 10 bottles were sold without being rung up. When staff know that inventory is strictly reconciled against the POS daily, the "no-ring" theft drops to zero.

## 4. The "Till Floating" Method

**How it works:**
Cashiers take cash out of the drawer for "petty cash" expenses (like buying extra tomatoes from the market) but don't record it, or they record a higher amount than was actually spent. This muddles the end-of-day cash reconciliation.

**How Dineiz stops it:**
Dineiz has a dedicated **Cash Management (Pay-in/Pay-out)** feature.
If money leaves the drawer, the cashier must punch it into the system, select a reason category (e.g., "Supplier Payment"), and print a receipt for it.

At the end of the shift, the system calculates:
`Starting Cash + Cash Sales - Pay Outs = Expected Drawer Amount`

If the physical cash does not match the Expected Drawer Amount exactly, the system flags a discrepancy.
