Bohat se dhaba owners yeh sochte hain ke billing software lagana sirf bare restaurants ka kaam hai. Unhe lagta hai ke is ke liye mehenge computers, IT walay larke, aur lakho rupay ki zaroorat hai.

Yeh bilkul ghalat hai.

Aaj ke daur mein, aap apne purane Android phone aur ek saste Daraz printer se apna poora dhaba digital kar sakte hain. Is guide mein hum aapko step-by-step batayenge ke apne dhaba mein billing software kaise start karein.

## Step 1: Sasta Hardware Khareedein

Aapko bhaari bharkam desktop PC lene ki koi zaroorat nahi. Aap in do cheezon se shuruwaat kar sakte hain:

### 1. Ek Android Phone ya Tablet
Aapke paas pehle se hi koi Android phone hoga. Agar nahi, tou koi bhi sasta used Android tablet (jaise Samsung Tab A series ya koi local brand) 15,000 se 20,000 PKR mein mil jata hai. Dineiz cloud par chalta hai, is liye device ki speed ka zyada farq nahi parta.

### 2. Bluetooth Thermal Printer
Computer walon ki baaton mein aakar mehenga printer mat lein. Daraz par "58mm Bluetooth Thermal Printer" search karein. Yeh chotay black-and-white printers 3,500 se 5,000 PKR ke darmiyan asani se mil jatay hain. Yeh direct aapke phone se Bluetooth ke zariye connect hote hain.

## Step 2: Apna Menu Setup Karein

Jab aapka hardware ready ho, tou next step apna menu software mein daalna hai.

Dhaba ka menu usually simple hota hai:
*   Chai (Doodh Patti, Peshawari Kahwa)
*   Paratha (Aloo, Keema, Sada)
*   Salan (Chicken Karahi, Daal Mash)
*   Roti / Naan

Dineiz mein, aap apne mobile se hi yeh items add kar sakte hain. Har item ki price set karein. Aap categories bhi bana sakte hain (jaise "Nashta" aur "Dinner") taake rush ke waqt order punch karna asaan ho.

## Step 3: Pehla Order Punch Karein

Ab aapka system ready hai. Jab customer order de:

1.  Dineiz app open karein.
2.  "Chai" par 2 dafa tap karein (2 chai ke liye).
3.  "Aloo Paratha" par 1 dafa tap karein.
4.  "Print Receipt" dabayein.

Bluetooth printer fauran ek saaf, professional parchi nikal dega jis par aapke dhaba ka naam, time, items, aur total bill likha hoga.

## Step 4: Rozana Hisaab (End of Day)

Yeh software lagane ka sab se bara faida hai.

Raat ko jab dhaba band karne ka time ho, tou aapko kaapi/register par ghanton hisaab karne ki zaroorat nahi. App mein "Shift Close" ka button dabayein.

System aapko fauran bata dega:
*   Aaj total kitni chai biki?
*   Galle (cash drawer) mein kitne paise hone chahiye?
*   Kitne orders cancel ya void hue?

Agar aap khud counter par nahi bethte aur staff rakha hua hai, tou yeh software aapko har order ka poora hisaab dikhata hai. Aap ghar bethe apne phone par dekh sakte hain ke aaj kitni sale hui hai.
