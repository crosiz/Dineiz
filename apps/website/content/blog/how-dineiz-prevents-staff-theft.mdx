# How Dineiz Prevents Staff Theft in Restaurants

Internal staff theft and cash drawer leaks account for up to 5% lost revenue in Pakistani restaurants every month. Common tricks include voiding orders after collecting cash, giving unauthorized discounts to friends, or stealing raw inventory items.

Dineiz POS prevents internal theft through four strict automated security protocols:

1. **Manager PIN Void Authorization:** Cashiers cannot void an order or item after printing a receipt without a manager entering a secure security PIN.
2. **Immutable Audit Logs:** Every order change, discount, or cash drawer opening leaves a permanent timestamped entry in the audit report.
3. **Recipe-based Raw Stock Reconciliation:** Deducts raw ingredients automatically so missing inventory becomes immediately obvious during stock counts.
4. **End-of-Shift Blind Reconciliation:** Cashiers must count cash blindly before seeing expected totals, preventing cash drawer adjustments.
