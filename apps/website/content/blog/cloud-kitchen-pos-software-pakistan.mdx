Cloud kitchens (also known as dark kitchens, ghost kitchens, or virtual food brands) are revolutionizing the food and beverage industry in Pakistan. 

With prime commercial real estate in areas like Gulberg (Lahore), Clifton & DHA (Karachi), and F-10/I-8 (Islamabad) becoming prohibitively expensive, ambitious restaurateurs are launching multiple delivery-first food brands out of a single, centralized commissary kitchen.

A single facility might prepare:
- A gourmet smash burger brand
- A wood-fired Neapolitan pizza brand
- A traditional biryani & pulao brand
- A dessert & waffle concept

However, operating multiple virtual brands from one kitchen with conventional restaurant billing software quickly turns into an operational nightmare. Receipts get mixed up, packaging is put in the wrong brand’s boxes, aggregators take 25% to 35% commission on every order, and tracking shared raw ingredients is virtually impossible.

In this guide, we review what Pakistani cloud kitchen operators need from their POS infrastructure in 2026, and how **Dineiz** solves the specific operational bottlenecks of virtual kitchens.

## 4. Shared Raw Ingredient Inventory & Recipe Costing

One of the biggest financial advantages of a multi-brand cloud kitchen is shared inventory. Your smash burger brand and your fried chicken brand might both use:
- Fresh poultry breast
- Canola cooking oil
- Mayonnaise and dipping sauces
- 500ml beverage cans

If your POS software treats each brand as an isolated silo, you cannot accurately reconcile how much chicken or oil was consumed by which brand.

### Dineiz Shared Inventory Intelligence:
- **Central Commissary Stock**: Track raw bulk ingredients in your central store.
- **Recipe Deductions**: When a *Double Smash Burger* is sold under Brand A, Dineiz automatically decrements 2 beef patties, 1 brioche bun, and 1 sheet of butter paper. When an item is sold under Brand B, it decrements from the same shared pool.
- **Actual vs. Theoretical Food Cost**: Detect wastage, pilferage, and portion control deviations across shifts.
