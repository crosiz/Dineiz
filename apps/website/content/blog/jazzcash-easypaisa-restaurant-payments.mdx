# JazzCash and EasyPaisa for Restaurant Payments — Complete Setup Guide

Digital mobile wallets have transformed consumer payments in Pakistan. Over 60% of smartphone users rely on JazzCash and EasyPaisa for everyday purchases.

For restaurants, offering fast digital wallet checkout reduces cash handling risks and speeds up counter transactions.

---

## Setting Up Digital Wallet Checkout on Dineiz POS

1. Open **Dineiz Console > Payment Settings**.
2. Enter your **JazzCash Merchant Till ID** or **EasyPaisa Business Till ID**.
3. Select *Enable Dynamic QR Generation*.

Once configured, Dineiz POS displays a dynamic QR code on the screen during checkout showing the exact bill total. Customers scan the QR on their phone app, pay instantly, and the POS automatically marks the bill as paid!
