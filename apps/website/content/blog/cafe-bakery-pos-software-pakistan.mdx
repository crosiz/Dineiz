## 1. Lightning-Fast Modifier Management (Sub-5-Second Punching)

In a specialty coffee shop, almost no drink is ordered "plain":

- *“Iced Spanish Latte, oat milk, half sweet, extra espresso shot, light ice.”*
- *“Hot Flat White, almond milk, decaf beans, vanilla syrup pump.”*

Generic POS software built for clothing stores or general retail forces the cashier to click through 6 different dropdown menus or type manual notes for every drink. During morning or post-dinner rush hours, this creates agonizing queues at your counter.

### How Dineiz Solves It:
- **One-Tap Modifier Groups**: Milk choices (Dairy, Oat, Almond, Coconut), sweetness levels (25%, 50%, 75%, 100%), temperature (Hot, Iced), and syrups are accessible right on the product screen with a single tap.
- **Modifier Pricing**: Dineiz automatically adds extra charges (e.g., +Rs. 150 for Oat Milk, +Rs. 120 for an extra shot) directly to the invoice without manual price overrides.
- **Printed Barista Tickets & KDS**: Drink customization prints clearly on dedicated cup labels or appears instantly on the barista’s Kitchen Display Screen (KDS).

## 2. Separate Routing for Barista Drinks vs. Kitchen Pastries

In a modern cafe, an order often includes both a beverage and food item—for example, an iced matcha latte and a toasted smoked chicken croissant.

If your POS prints a single kitchen slip to one thermal printer:
1. The barista has to grab the receipt from the kitchen cook, or
2. The kitchen cook has to wait for the barista before preparing the food.

### The Dineiz Multi-Station Solution:
Dineiz supports **intelligent station routing**. When an order is punched:
- The beverage items automatically route to the **Barista Station / Barista KDS**.
- The pastry and kitchen items automatically route to the **Bakery Kitchen KDS / Printer**.
- Both stations work simultaneously, cutting order fulfillment time in half.

## 3. Fresh Bakes, Batch Expiry & Markdown Management for Bakeries

Bakeries face a perishable inventory dilemma. Fresh croissants, artisanal sourdough, brownies, and pastries baked at 7:00 AM lose value if not sold by the end of the evening.

Key bakery requirements include:
- **Daily Batch Production**: Recording how many batches of croissants or cakes were baked each morning.
- **Evening Clearance & Happy Hour Rules**: Automatically applying a 20% or 30% discount on fresh baked items after 8:00 PM without cashiers manually editing prices.
- **Pre-Orders & Custom Cake Bookings**: Accepting advance deposits (e.g., 50% upfront via JazzCash or credit card) for custom birthday cakes, with automated reminders when pickup time arrives.

Dineiz includes built-in batch tracking and scheduled automatic price rules, letting you sell out fresh stock profitably while stopping unauthorized manual discounts.

## 5. Local Pakistani Tax Compliance (FBR, PRA, SRB & Split GST)

Hospitality tax compliance in Pakistan requires automated handling of differential GST rates:

- **Punjab (PRA)**: 5% GST on credit/debit card payments vs. 16% on cash payments.
- **Sindh (SRB)**: 8% or 13% depending on restaurant category.
- **Federal (FBR)**: Direct integration with POS invoicing and mandatory QR codes printed on thermal customer receipts.

Dineiz is **pre-configured for Pakistani tax laws**. The cashier never has to do mental math:
- When the customer taps their Visa or Mastercard, Dineiz automatically applies the compliant reduced card GST rate.
- When paying cash, Dineiz adjusts to the standard cash GST rate.
- Receipts include the required FBR / provincial QR code and invoice number automatically.

## 6. Ingredient-Level Inventory & Recipe Costing (Controlling Bean & Dairy Costs)

With continuous fluctuations in coffee bean import prices, milk, whipping cream, and imported chocolate, cafe profit margins can vanish quickly without precise recipe control.

Dineiz lets you define exact bills of materials (BOM):
- 1 Large Cappuccino = 18g specialty espresso beans + 220ml whole milk + 1 disposable paper cup + 1 sipping lid.
- Every time a large cappuccino is punched, Dineiz decrements 18 grams of beans and 220ml of milk from your live stock.
- When milk or bean stocks drop below your safety threshold, you get instant low-stock alerts on your mobile phone.
