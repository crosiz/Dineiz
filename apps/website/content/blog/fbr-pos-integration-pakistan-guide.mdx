# FBR POS Integration for Restaurants in Pakistan — Complete Guide 2025

Operating a tier-1 or registered restaurant in Pakistan requires strict compliance with the **Federal Board of Revenue (FBR)** POS integration mandate. Failure to issue real-time FBR POS invoices can result in heavy penalties and operational audits.

In this guide, we break down everything Pakistani restaurant owners need to know about FBR POS invoicing in 2025.

---

## 1. What is FBR POS Integration?

FBR POS Integration is a digital compliance system that connects your restaurant point-of-sale software directly with FBR fiscal servers. Whenever a customer bill is generated, the invoice details (subtotal, tax rate, NTN, STRN) are sent to FBR instantly, returning a unique **FBR Invoice Number** and **FBR QR Code** to be printed on the paper receipt.

---

## 2. Applicable GST Tax Rates for Pakistani Restaurants

Tax rates vary depending on whether the sale is dine-in, takeaway, or delivery, as well as the provincial tax authority jurisdiction:

* **Federal (FBR):** 5% GST on card payments at integrated restaurants, 15% on cash payments.
* **Punjab (PRA):** 5% PST on dining services.
* **Sindh (SRB):** 8% or 13% depending on registration category.
* **KPK (KPRA):** 5% PST for registered eating establishments.
