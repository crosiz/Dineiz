# Case Study: Gulshan Restaurant Group

Gulshan Restaurant Group operates 3 dining branches in Gulberg, DHA, and Johar Town in Lahore. 

Prior to implementing Dineiz, management struggled to get accurate sales reports across branches without visiting each location physically.

## Multi-Branch Control with Dineiz Console

With **Dineiz Console** and **Dineiz POS**:

* **Live Sales Visibility:** Management monitors real-time sales, top dishes, and table occupancy from any browser.
* **Kitchen Efficiency:** Digital KDS screens eliminated lost paper KOT slips and reduced kitchen fulfillment times to under 12 minutes.
* **FBR Tax Compliance:** Automatic generation of FBR QR code receipts simplified provincial tax reporting.
