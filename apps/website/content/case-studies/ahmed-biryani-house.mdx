# Case Study: Ahmed Biryani House

Ahmed Biryani House is a popular counter-service eatery in Karachi serving over 400 plates of biryani daily. 

Before adopting Dineiz, owner Tariq Mahmood managed operations using a manual paper register. During lunch rush hours, hand-written bills caused long customer queues and frequent calculation errors.

## The Transformation with Dineiz Go & WhatsApp AI

By downloading **Dineiz Go** on an Android tablet and pairing a Bluetooth thermal printer:

* **Speed of Service:** Order punch time dropped from 45 seconds to just 6 seconds.
* **Automated WhatsApp Delivery:** The Dineiz WhatsApp AI Bot handled late-night delivery orders automatically, collecting payment via JazzCash QR.
* **Zero Discrepancies:** Daily cashier reconciliation eliminated cash drawer leaks.
